# Divergence axes

Hit every axis. The ones that feel unproductive are the ones that earn their place — an idea set that
is all Axis 1 and 2 is a list, not a brainstorm.

| # | Axis | Prompt to yourself |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Obvious** | What would any competent person propose first? Name it so it stops occupying attention. |
| 2 | **Ambitious** | What if resources were not the constraint? |
| 3 | **10x simpler** | What is the version that takes a weekend? What if we deleted the hardest requirement entirely? |
| 4 | **Contrarian** | What does the default approach get wrong? What would we do if the popular choice were banned? |
| 5 | **Premise-questioning** | Is the stated problem the real problem? What if the user should not build this at all? |
| 6 | **Horizon bet** `#horizon-bet` | What changes in 12-24 months — model capability, unit costs, a platform shift, a regulation — that makes an idea newly viable or newly dead? Which idea is a bet on that curve? |
| 7 | **Steal** | Who has solved a structurally identical problem in another domain? Name them. |
| 8 | **Constraint flip** | Turn the binding constraint into the feature. Slow becomes deliberate; small becomes focused. |
| 9 | **Failure-first** | Assume it launched and nobody used it. Why? The fix to that is an idea. |
| 10 | **Compounding** | Which idea makes the *next* three ideas cheaper? Leverage beats local optimum. |

## Quality bar

- An idea is one sentence a stranger could repeat. If you need a paragraph, it is a theme.
- "Add AI" / "make it faster" / "improve UX" are categories, not ideas. Push to the specific move.
- Two ideas that differ only in wording are one idea. Merge and move on.
- Argue against your own favourite once, in writing. It survives or it does not.

## Scoring

`impact` `effort` `confidence`, each 1-5, `effort` 5 = expensive.

Sort by leverage, not by score sum. Useful reads:
- **high impact / low effort** → the roadmap's Phase 1 candidates.
- **high impact / low confidence** → not a task, a *spike*. Name what would raise confidence.
- **low impact / low effort** → `horizon: someday`. Never let these crowd out the top of a phase.
- **high effort / high confidence** → real work; must be broken down before it can be committed.

Do not average the three into one number. The shape of the triple is the information.
